## Conditions to avail ITC Section 16(2)

- Possession of Invoice, Debit Note
- Returns have to actually furnished under section 39
- Should received goods or Services or both
- Tax of supply has been actually paid to the government

## ITC Reversal Rule 42

T = Total ITC
t1 = ITC for non business use
t2 = ITC for Exempt supplies
t3 = Blocked ITC
t4 = Taxable Supplies
Tc = Common Credit = T - (T1 + T2 + T3 + T4)
D1 = Common ITC for Exempt Supplies; D2 = (E/F) * Tc 
D2 = Reversal for non- business use; D2 = 5% of Tc (if Applicable)

C2 = Net Eligible
Net Eligible ITC = T- (t1+t2+t3+d1+d2)
